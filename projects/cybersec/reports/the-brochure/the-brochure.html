The Brochure

OSINT investigation of a promotional brochure leading to a hidden social media account and a concealed flag.

Description

The objective of this challenge was to identify a hidden connection left within a promotional image published by the fictional Byte Lotus Hotel.

The investigation focused on open-source intelligence techniques, including image analysis and social media attribution, in order to identify the hidden account and recover the challenge flag.

1. Reconnaissance

Initial Analysis

The provided challenge consisted of a single promotional image used in the hotel's brochure.

Based on the challenge description, it was reasonable to assume that the image itself contained hidden indicators that could be used to identify the original source or the creator of the content.

Enumeration

Tools and techniques used during the initial investigation:

  • ExifTool
  • Manual image inspection
  • Social media search
  • Instagram
  • CyberChef

Metadata analysis did not reveal any useful information. Further investigation therefore focused on identifying visual characteristics and tracing the image back to its original online source.

2. Investigation

Findings

During manual inspection of the brochure, two significant indicators were identified.

The first suggested the existence of an official Instagram presence for the resort. The second introduced the virtual concierge VERA, which appeared to represent another potential investigation target.

Methodology

The investigation followed a standard OSINT workflow. Initial analysis focused on extracting information directly from the provided asset.

After identifying references to social media, publicly available Instagram accounts related to the resort were enumerated. Relationship analysis between publicly visible accounts was then performed in order to identify additional entities connected to the resort.

Further inspection of the discovered profile revealed multiple Base64-encoded fragments distributed across several public posts.

After reconstructing the complete encoded string, CyberChef was used to decode the final value, revealing the challenge flag.

3. Conclusion

Result

The investigation successfully revealed the hidden information left by the creator of the Byte Lotus Resort assets.

4. Security Implications

This challenge demonstrates how publicly available information may unintentionally expose sensitive data through OSINT techniques.

Even when no technical vulnerability exists, publicly accessible relationships between accounts, images, and published content can reveal information that was not intended to be easily discoverable.

Organizations should carefully review published material, associated social media accounts, and publicly visible relationships before publication.

5. Lessons Learned

  • OSINT investigations often begin with simple visual inspection.
  • Small clues may become valuable pivot points during an investigation.
  • Social media relationship analysis is an effective attribution technique.
  • Base64 encoding should never be considered a security mechanism.
  • Multiple independent OSINT techniques can be combined to reach a single objective.
