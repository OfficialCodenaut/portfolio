Nynäsbanan

Railway simulator on Roblox inspired by the real-life Nynäsbanan railway line in Sweden.

Roblox Lua/Luau Simulation Systems

Overview

Nynäsbanan is a railway simulation project built on Roblox, inspired by the real-life Nynäsbanan railway line between Stockholm and Nynäshamn.

The project focuses on building a realistic train simulation from the ground up, combining railway infrastructure, train systems, multiplayer networking, and interactive driving mechanics.

Rather than relying entirely on Roblox's built-in physics, the project uses custom systems to control train movement, track following, consists, and player interaction.

Features

Custom Track System

Train movement is based on a custom spline-driven track system designed to follow railway geometry.

Train Consists

The system supports configurable train consists, including both 6-car and 12-car SL X60 configurations.

Cabin Camera

A dedicated driver's camera provides an immersive first-person view from inside the train cab.

Driver Assignment

Players can be assigned to operate trains through the simulation's multiplayer systems.

Server-Authoritative Systems

Core train state and movement are handled server-side to maintain consistent multiplayer state.

Railway Infrastructure

The project recreates railway infrastructure and environments inspired by the real Nynäsbanan line.

Development

The project is developed as a three-person team, with a focus on building the underlying simulation systems rather than simply creating a visual railway environment.

One of the main technical challenges is representing railway movement in a way that remains smooth and predictable across curves, different train configurations, and multiplayer interactions.

The architecture is being developed around server-authoritative train state, custom track geometry, and modular systems that can be extended as the simulation grows.
